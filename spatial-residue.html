06 / Reading
CRITICAL READING
"Architecture as the container of the unresolved. Spatial logic at the edge of dream."
Four thematic readings
I
Architecture as uncanny container
Not spaces for dwelling but for passing through. Marc Augé's non-place: the airport, the motorway, the hotel corridor. Translated into a visual language closer to dream-logic than sociological taxonomy.
II
Music cover as surrogate architecture
Album covers here do not illustrate the music / they are its spatial imagination made visible. The cover as portal, a frame around a space you can hear but never enter.
III
The body as intrusion
Where figures appear, the body reads not as subject but as scale device, a ghost haunting a space that doesn't need it. The spaces preexist the inhabitants and will persist after them.
IV
Y2K and the aestheticisation of rationality
1997–2004: smooth curves, brushed steel, ice-blue whites, acid greens. A moment when the rational grid was simultaneously celebrated and dissolved into sensation.
